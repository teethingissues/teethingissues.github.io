Questions

Real answers.

What is Teething Issues?

Teething Issues is a UK practice support company for dental practices. Its founder is a dental care professional, a CQC expert, a former CQC Registered Manager and a growth business consultant. Teething Issues installs three operating systems in a dental practice: the Growth OS, the Compliance OS and the Operations OS. The Growth OS answers every call and follows up every enquiry and treatment plan. The Compliance OS keeps a practice calm and in order for the Care Quality Commission (CQC) all year, with 481 checks across 20 departments. The Operations OS handles rebooking, hiring, onboarding and admin. Each system goes in one at a time, with the Teething Issues team working side by side with the practice team until it is second nature. For example, a practice can start with the Growth OS and add the Compliance OS later. The practice owner stays in charge of every decision.

Who is Teething Issues for?

Teething Issues is for owners and practice managers of UK dental practices who want a calmer, better-run practice. Common signs a practice is a good fit: calls going unanswered at lunch or after five, consultations that don’t turn into treatment, gaps in the diary, staff paperwork that keeps slipping, or worry about the next CQC inspection. The founder screens every practice before starting, because the Teething Issues name sits behind every practice it supports. The first step is a free 30-minute chat about the practice’s numbers. Rough numbers are fine, and there is no obligation to go ahead. For example, a practice manager can bring last month’s missed calls and new patient numbers, and leave the chat knowing which system would help most.

What is a dental practice operating system?

A dental practice operating system is a joined-up set of tools, checklists and routines that runs one part of a practice the same way every day. Teething Issues builds three. First, the Growth OS covers the patient journey from the first phone call to treatment booked: every call answered, every enquiry followed up, every treatment plan tracked, and conversion measured, aiming for 70%. Second, the Compliance OS covers Care Quality Commission standards: documents read line by line, staff training and certificates tracked, and every gap given an owner and a date. Third, the Operations OS covers the running of the practice: failed appointments and cancellations rebooked, hiring and onboarding, and the inbox. Each operating system shows the owner simple numbers, so progress is clear each day rather than at month end.

How does Teething Issues help a dental practice get ready for CQC?

The Care Quality Commission (CQC) inspects dental practices in England against five key questions: are services safe, effective, caring, responsive and well-led? The Teething Issues Compliance OS checks a practice against those standards with 481 checks across 20 departments, including medical emergencies, COSHH, decontamination, staff files and equipment servicing. A former CQC Registered Manager reads the practice’s real documents line by line, rather than ticking a template. For example, the staff file check flags a DBS certificate that isn’t Enhanced or is more than 3 years old, and a Hepatitis B record with no blood test result. Every gap is given an owner and a date, so the practice stays ready for an inspection any day, instead of rushing when the letter arrives.

How does Teething Issues stop missed calls at a dental practice?

Missed calls are calls that ring out because the front desk is busy, at lunch, or closed after five. Teething Issues adds a receptionist that answers every call the team can’t get to, day and night, in multiple languages. The receptionist follows the practice’s own rules, knows each dentist and how long each treatment takes, spots emergencies, never gives medical advice, and sends the team a short summary of every call. Then every missed call and every treatment plan that went quiet is followed up. Practices can hear it first: ringing 020 4572 1391 reaches a demo set up as a made-up practice, Willow Tree Dental. To work out what missed calls cost a practice, multiply missed calls a week by how many of those callers would have booked, then by what a new patient usually spends.

Does Teething Issues work with Dentally and Software of Excellence?

Yes. Teething Issues works alongside the practice management software a dental practice already uses, including Dentally and Software of Excellence (SOE). Practices do not need to switch systems. During the free 30-minute chat, Teething Issues checks which software the practice uses and how the team books appointments, so the receptionist, follow-ups and reports fit around the existing setup. The practice keeps full control of its own diary and patient records.

Will Teething Issues replace my staff?

No. Teething Issues never replaces a practice’s staff. The systems take repetitive work off the team, such as calls at lunch, chasing follow-ups and paperwork, so receptionists, nurses and managers get their breaks back and spend more time with patients. Training is part of the work: the Growth OS includes practice calls with real feedback, so the team gets better every week. Nothing big changes without the owner’s say.
