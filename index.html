Common Questions

Real answers, no vague marketing-speak.

How does it work?

It starts with a free 30-minute Practice Growth Review. We look at your numbers and what's slipping, agree the areas that matter most, and give you a clear plan with a fixed price. We set it up in about a week and stay with you for the first month.

What does it cost?

It depends on the areas you choose, so we give you a fixed price after your Growth Review. After the first month, a monthly care plan keeps it all running. No surprises: we always tell you the price first.

Am I tied in?

No. After the first month the care plan is month to month. Stay because it works.

How quickly will I see a difference?

Missed calls change in the first week. Conversion and the team build over the first month.

Will this replace my staff?

No. Nobody gets replaced. Everything we do is there to make your team's day easier.

Is our patient data safe?

Yes. We're registered with the ICO, work under UK GDPR, and agree a data processing agreement with every practice before we handle any patient details.

Why are you selective about which practices you take on?

Our name is attached to every practice we work with. We only take on practices we can genuinely help.
