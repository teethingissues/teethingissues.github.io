Questions

Real answers.

What is Teething Issues?

Teething Issues is a practice support company for UK dental practices, developed by dental care professionals. Teething Issues answers every call the team can’t get to, keeps CQC compliance standards high all year, hires, onboards, trains and tracks staff, runs a Sales Engine that finds and closes the leaks in a practice’s numbers, and grows visibility on Google, ChatGPT and social media. The founder is a former CQC Registered Manager with more than 15 years inside dental practices.

Who is Teething Issues for?

Teething Issues works with UK dental practice owners, practice managers and dental entrepreneurs, especially practices that are struggling to answer every call, not getting enough new patients, seeing low conversion on consultations, or feeling the stress of CQC. Practices choose the areas they want help with at a 30-minute growth chat.

What does it cost?

One service on its own (like the receptionist) is £1,500 to set up, then £150 a month. Packages: Starter (2 areas) £1,999, Growth (5 areas) £4,995, Complete (every area) £9,995, then a £300 a month care plan. We always tell you the price first.

Am I tied in?

No. There's no long contract, and you can cancel any time. Stay because it works.

How quickly will I see a difference?

Missed calls change in the first week. Conversion and the team build over the first month.

Will this replace my staff?

No. Nobody gets replaced. Everything we do is there to make your team's day easier.

Does it work with my practice software?

Yes. We work with Dentally, Software of Excellence (SOE) and other practice systems. We'll check yours at your growth chat.

Is our patient data safe?

Yes. We're registered with the ICO, work under UK GDPR, and agree a data processing agreement with every practice before we handle any patient details.

Why are you selective about which practices you take on?

Our name is attached to every practice we work with. We only take on practices we can genuinely help.
