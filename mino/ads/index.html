Who sees these ads

  • Where: United Kingdom.
  • Who: people whose job title or interests include dentist, dental practice owner, practice manager, dental nurse, dental business, CQC, or the British Dental Association. Age 25–65.
  • Platforms: Facebook and Instagram (feed, Stories and Reels). LinkedIn is an option later: very precise for practice owners, but roughly 5–10 times the cost per click.
  • Goal: waitlist sign-ups, tracked on the Mino page.
