Good to know

What is Mino?

Mino is an AI chief of staff for UK dental practices, made by Teething Issues. Practice owners talk to Mino, and Mino coordinates a team of specialist AI assistants for compliance, admin, training, reception and growth, telling you clearly which team a finding belongs to.

When is Mino launching?

Mino is coming in 2027. Waitlist practices will be the first to hear, the first to get access, and the first to help shape what Mino does. There's no cost to join the waitlist and no obligation.

Who is behind Mino?

Mino is made by Teething Issues, a team of dental professionals, CQC compliance experts and business growth experts who have worked inside real UK practices. Mino is built around the real jobs that keep practice owners up at night.
