What it does

Answers every call

When reception is busy, at lunch, after five and at weekends. No voicemail, no ringing out.

Takes the details

Name, number, new or existing patient and why they're calling, then a tidy summary to your team straight away.

Books patients in

On Dentally it can book straight into your diary. On other systems, like SOE, it offers a time and your team confirms it.

Spots emergencies

Pain, swelling or trauma are flagged to your team at once, and callers are pointed to 999 or 111 when they need it.

Knows your prices

Shares your "from" prices, always saying the dentist confirms the cost at the consultation. Never gives clinical advice.

Reminds patients the day before

A friendly reminder call before each appointment. Patients confirm, or ask for a new time and your team calls them back, so fewer empty chairs.

Never makes things up

It only gives answers from your own information. If it doesn't know, it says the team will check, and it never tells a patient something is booked until it really is.

Sounds like your practice

Answers with your practice name, in a warm, friendly voice, and helps your team rather than replacing anyone.

Questions

Will it replace my receptionist?

No. It picks up the calls your team can't get to, so they can focus on the patients in front of them.

Can it book appointments?

If you use Dentally, yes, straight into your diary. With other practice software it offers a time and your team confirms it in your system. We'll check yours at the Growth Review.

What if a patient asks something it doesn't know?

It says so, takes the question, and your team calls back with the answer. It never guesses prices or clinical details, and never confirms a booking or payment that hasn't actually happened.

Is patient data safe?

Yes. We're registered with the ICO, work under UK GDPR, and agree a data processing agreement with every practice. Callers are told calls are recorded.
