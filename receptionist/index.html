Questions

What is a dental receptionist service from Teething Issues?

The Teething Issues receptionist is a telephone receptionist for UK dental practices that answers the calls the practice team can’t get to, at lunch, after five and at busy times. The receptionist speaks multiple languages, spots dental emergencies, sends the team a summary of every call, and only answers from what the practice has taught it. The service costs £1,500 to set up, then £150 a month, with no long contract.

How much do missed calls cost a dental practice?

Missed calls cost dental practices new patients and treatment. Around 1 in 3 calls to dental practices go unanswered at busy times. Based on just 10 missed calls a week, Teething Issues estimates a practice can lose around £5,600 a month in treatment that never gets booked.

Will it replace my receptionist?

No. It picks up the calls your team can't get to, so they can focus on the patients in front of them.

Can it book appointments?

If you use Dentally, yes, straight into your diary. With other practice software it offers a time and your team confirms it in your system. We'll check yours at your growth chat.

What if a patient asks something it doesn't know?

It says so, takes the question, and your team calls back with the answer. It never guesses prices or clinical details, and never confirms a booking or payment that hasn't actually happened.

Is patient data safe?

Yes. We're registered with the ICO, work under UK GDPR, and agree a data processing agreement with every practice. Callers are told calls are recorded.

How quickly can it start?

Usually within a week of your growth chat.
