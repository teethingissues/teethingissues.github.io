Questions

What is a dental receptionist service from Teething Issues?

The Teething Issues receptionist is a telephone receptionist for UK dental practices that answers the calls the practice team can’t get to: at lunch, after five, at weekends and whenever the front desk is full. It answers in a calm, natural voice, speaks multiple languages, spots dental emergencies straight away and books patients in. On Dentally it books straight into the diary; on Software of Excellence (SOE) and other systems it offers a time and the team confirms it. Every call is summed up for the team, and the owner gets the numbers on their phone each morning. It only answers from what the practice has taught it, never gives medical advice and never guesses prices. It picks up the overflow and never replaces the reception team. There is no long contract, and you can ring the demo on 020 4572 1391 to hear it for yourself.

How much do missed calls cost a dental practice?

Every missed call can be a new patient who books with the practice down the road instead, and most callers who reach voicemail don’t leave a message. To work out your own cost, take the number of calls missed in a week, times how many of those callers would have booked, times what a new patient usually spends with you in their first year. For example, a practice that misses 20 calls a week, where 1 in 4 would have booked and a new patient spends £400, is losing around £2,000 a week, or about £92,000 over a 46-week year. Missed calls cluster at lunch, after five and when the desk is busy with patients in front of them. The Teething Issues receptionist answers those calls, books the patient in and follows up any call that still gets missed, so the practice stops losing patients it never knew it had.

Will it replace my receptionist?

No. The Teething Issues receptionist is built to support the reception team, never to replace it. It picks up the calls the team can’t get to, at lunch, after five and when they are busy with patients at the desk, so nobody has to choose between the patient in front of them and the phone. The team gets a tidy summary of every call, with who rang, what they wanted and what was booked, so nothing is missed when they pick things back up. Reception teams usually find their day gets calmer: breaks are actually taken, the phone stops ringing over conversations, and they have more time for the patient journey. The practice owner stays in charge of what the receptionist says and does, and it only answers from what the practice has taught it.

Can it book appointments?

Yes. If the practice uses Dentally, the Teething Issues receptionist books appointments straight into the diary, using the right dentist and the right length for each treatment. With Software of Excellence (SOE) and other practice systems, it offers the patient a time and the team confirms it in the practice software, so nothing is booked that the team can’t see. It never confirms a booking or a payment that hasn’t actually happened. It can also call patients who failed to attend and rebook them, and fill last-minute cancellations, so gaps in the diary are filled before they cost the practice. We check which software you use when we chat, and set the booking up to match how your practice already works.

What if a patient asks something it doesn’t know?

It says so honestly, takes the question and the patient’s details, and your team calls back with the answer. The Teething Issues receptionist only answers from what the practice has taught it, so it never makes information up, never guesses prices or clinical details, and never gives medical advice. If a caller describes a dental emergency, such as swelling, bleeding that won’t stop or severe pain, it flags it straight away so the team can act. It never confirms a booking or a payment that hasn’t actually happened. Every question it couldn’t answer appears in the call summary, so the practice can teach it the answer for next time and it gets better every week.

Is patient data safe?

Yes. Teething Issues Ltd is registered with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO registration C2051112), works under UK GDPR and agrees a data processing agreement with every practice before the receptionist goes live. Callers are told that calls are recorded, and recordings and call summaries are only used to run the practice’s phones and improve the service for that practice. Patient information is kept to the minimum needed to book or pass on a message, access is limited to the people doing the work, and nothing is sold or used for marketing. The receptionist never gives medical advice and never takes card payments over the phone. If a practice stops using the receptionist, its data is returned or deleted as agreed in the data processing agreement.

First we learn how your practice works: your dentists, how long each treatment takes, your opening hours, your prices if you want them shared, and what counts as an emergency. Then we set up your phone line so the receptionist picks up the calls your team can’t get to, at lunch, after five or whenever the desk is busy. Before it goes live, you ring it yourself and try it as a patient, and nothing changes until you’re happy. Once it’s live, your team gets a summary of every call and you get your numbers each morning. There is no long contract, so if it isn’t right for your practice, you can stop at any time.
