Who sees these ads

  • Where: United Kingdom.
  • Who: dental practice owners, practice managers, associate dentists and senior dental nurses (job titles and interests: dentist, dental practice, practice manager, CQC, British Dental Association). Age 25–65.
  • Platforms: Facebook and Instagram (feed, Stories and Reels).
  • Goal: people joining the Circle on Skool. Ad link (the landing page, which warms them up with the free CQC score and ChatGPT test before they join): teethingissues.com/circle. The tracking tags on the link show which joins came from the ads.

Before the ads go live

  • Ad link: teethingissues.com/circle (with tracking tags). Every ad's button goes here; the page then sends people to Skool. For the Instagram and Facebook bio use the plain teethingissues.com/circle.
  • Price (decided 1 Oct): free to join (Circle Lite). Premium: first 20 founding members free, then £19/month (about $25) for the next 30, rising as the Circle grows (final price not announced). Skool payouts connected 2 Oct: all 6 courses in full, every template and the monthly live Q&A.

  • Welcome post: post it before anyone arrives, so the community isn't empty.
