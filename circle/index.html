Questions

Is Teething Issues Circle free?

Yes. Teething Issues Circle is free to join on Skool, and free members get the community and the core courses. Premium adds Mino-powered tools such as the AI Visibility Check & Fix and, once the Circle reaches 50 members, the Staff Paperwork Audit. The first 20 founding members get Premium free, and the price then rises in steps as the Circle grows.

Who is the Circle for?

Teething Issues Circle is a community for people running or building UK dental practices: practice owners and practice managers, associate and principal dentists, dentists planning to open their own practice, and senior dental nurses and team members. It focuses on 4 areas: CQC compliance, team management, patient growth and practical AI. For example, a dentist planning to open a practice can use the Inspection Ready course to set up staff files and audits correctly from day one.

How do I unlock the level rewards?

Every like on your posts and comments in the Circle is a point. Level 2 (5 likes) unlocks Spark for Dentists and the Practice Growth Engine, level 3 (20 likes) unlocks Jarvis for Dentists, and level 4 (65 likes) gives you free Premium forever. The first member to reach level 6 wins every Teething Issues resource, forever.

Do the Premium tools need technical skills?

No. Each tool is a small file you add to the Claude app on your own computer, with a five-minute guide. You then ask in plain English, for example "audit this staff file" or "check my website's AI visibility", and Mino does the work and writes a simple report.

Is my practice's data safe?

Yes. Every Teething Issues Premium tool is a Claude skill that runs on your own computer, in your own Claude account. Staff documents, CQC evidence and practice files are never uploaded to Teething Issues or to Skool. For example, the Staff Paperwork Audit reads a staff file on your own laptop and writes its Yes/No report there too.

Who runs Teething Issues Circle?

Teething Issues was started by a dental professional who began as a dental nurse, went on to manage practices, became a CQC Registered Manager, and helped double a practice's revenue to £135k a month. Everything in the Circle comes from real practices and real inspections.
