Close the Loop: Audit Actions Log and Gap Response Checklist

Use the first two sections to check your audits now and keep the actions log going after every audit. Keep the gap response steps to hand for inspection day, so the team knows exactly what to do if an inspector finds something.

Audit loop check (infection control and radiographs)

  • ☐ Last two infection control audits are on file, not just the latest
  • ☐ Last two radiograph audits are on file, not just the latest
  • ☐ Each audit has an action plan attached
  • ☐ Each action has a named owner and a deadline
  • ☐ Re-audit booked 3–6 months after the audit. Date: ________
  • ☐ Re-audit result recorded and compared with the previous score

Evidence chain check (clinical and safeguarding areas)

  • Area being checked: ____________________
  • ☐ Requirement identified
  • ☐ Policy in place and current
  • ☐ Training delivered and recorded
  • ☐ Staff can explain it when asked
  • ☐ Records kept
  • ☐ Audited, with findings, actions and re-audit logged

Actions log (copy one line per finding)

  • Finding: ____________________
  • Owner: ____________________
  • Deadline: ____ / ____ / ______
  • Done date: ____ / ____ / ______
  • Evidence of the fix filed at: ____________________

If an inspector finds a gap

  • ☐ Acknowledge it openly, without being defensive
  • ☐ Fix it promptly (for example, sign the risk assessment or book testing with named dates)
  • ☐ Document the fix with a date
  • ☐ Be ready to show the evidence
  • ☐ Send any evidence you already had but didn't produce on the day
