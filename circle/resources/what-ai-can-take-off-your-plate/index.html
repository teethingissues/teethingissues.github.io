Which type is each job? (tick one per job)

  • ☐ Checking: spotting what's missing, expired or likely to be flagged (e.g. staff files, audits, risk assessments)
  • ☐ Reminding: making sure something happens on time
  • ☐ Drafting: writing a first version of emails, messages or documents
  • ☐ Chasing: following up on replies, paperwork or outstanding tasks
  • ☐ Decision: needs a person to judge or approve (AI supports, people decide)

Where could AI help in our practice? (tick any that fit)

  • ☐ Compliance checking across staff files, audits and risk assessments
  • ☐ Handling routine calls, emails and diary admin, flagging only what needs a human
  • ☐ Drafting replies and messages for a team member to check
  • ☐ Outreach or follow-up emails that are carefully checked before going out
  • ☐ One calm point of contact for admin, with specialists behind it

Keeping people in charge

  • ☐ We have agreed who reviews anything AI checks, drafts or chases
  • ☐ We know which decisions always stay with a person
  • ☐ The team understands AI is there to take jobs off stretched people, not replace them
