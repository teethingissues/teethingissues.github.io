Inspector Questions Team Practice Sheet

Use this at a team meeting or a quiet five minutes. Ask each question, let the person answer in their own words (no scripts), then tick if they covered the key points or note what to revisit.

Before the session

  • ☐ Our Radiation Protection Supervisor is: ____________________
  • ☐ Our safeguarding lead is: ____________________
  • ☐ Our first aider is: ____________________
  • ☐ Emergency kit location (exact place): ____________________ and it is signposted

Receptionist questions

  • ☐ Duty of Candour: covers apologising to the person and offering support or a remedy to put it right where we can
  • ☐ Emergency kit: names the exact place and knows it is signposted
  • ☐ Safeguarding: knows where the contact details and flowchart are, says 999 if someone is in immediate danger, knows social services can be contacted
  • ☐ Empty reception: nothing left on the desk, screens locked, papers shredded, and ready for "show me your desk right now"
  • ☐ Patients in pain: explains we hold emergency slots and never turn a patient in pain away

Dental nurse questions

  • ☐ Decontamination: closed, lidded transport box, never hand-handle sharp instruments, follows the decon room poster for PPE, same process every day, and ready to "show me"
  • ☐ Autoclave validation: daily checks confirm it reaches 134°C and holds for 2 minutes, logged every day, serviced every year, and can show today's log
  • ☐ Wrong-site treatment: confirms name and date of birth, shows the patient which tooth, checks the treatment plan, gets verbal and written consent
  • ☐ Radiation Protection Supervisor: can give the name straight away
  • ☐ Needlestick from a high-risk patient: knows where the sharps injury flowchart is, including assessing the source patient's risk

After the session

  • ☐ Question that tripped us up: ____________________
  • ☐ Who needs a refresher, and on what: ____________________
  • ☐ Date we will re-run this round: ____________________
