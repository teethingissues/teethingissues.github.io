CQC Inspection Readiness Planner: Notice to Final Report

Print this and work through it with your Registered Manager now, while there's no inspection date hanging over you. Keep it somewhere the team can find it, so if the call comes you already know who does what and when.

If the call came tomorrow: who does what

  • Person who takes the lead inspector's call: ____________
  • Person who uploads documents to the portal: ____________
  • Back-up if they're on leave or off sick: ____________
  • ☐ Portal login details are known by both people above
  • ☐ Team know there's usually about 2 weeks' written notice, and unannounced visits are rare

Key documents ready to upload within a few days

  • ☐ Statement of Purpose (current and accurate) – stored at: ____________
  • ☐ Latest infection control audit – date completed: ____________
  • ☐ Legionella risk assessment – date completed: ____________
  • ☐ All three saved in one folder that both named people can access

Your 10–15 minute opening talk: notes

  • Practice history: ____________
  • Opening hours: ____________
  • NHS and private mix: ____________
  • Team (who's who, roles): ____________
  • Special treatments offered: ____________
  • Community work: ____________
  • What we do well: ____________
  • What we want to improve: ____________

On the day and after

  • ☐ Reception, nurses and dentists know the inspector and Specialist Dental Advisor may speak to them directly
  • ☐ Clinical team know the advisor will spend time in surgeries and the decon room, watching and asking questions
  • ☐ Diary note: check the draft report and flag factual mistakes within the 17-working-day window
  • ☐ Diary note: improvement plan (if needed) usually due within about 2 weeks of the final report
  • ☐ Team understand one breach in one area means "Not all regulations met" – aim to be solid everywhere
