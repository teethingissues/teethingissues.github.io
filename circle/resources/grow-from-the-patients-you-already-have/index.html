Grow From Your Own Patient List: Planning Checklist

Work through this with your practice manager or reception lead before you send anything. Tick each item as you go, and don't send any marketing until the consent section is complete.

Step 1: Check your marketing consent first

  • ☐ We know where marketing consent is recorded in our software
  • ☐ We can tell which patients have agreed to hear from us, and by which method (email, text, post)
  • ☐ We've checked our consent records cover the messages we plan to send
  • ☐ Anything we're unsure about has been checked before sending
  • ☐ Marketing goes only to patients who've agreed to hear from us
  • Who's responsible for checking consent: ____________________

Step 2: Patients with missing teeth or dentures

  • ☐ Asked our software supplier how to pull a list of patients with extractions, gaps or dentures
  • ☐ List pulled and filtered to patients with marketing consent only
  • Number of patients on the list: ________
  • ☐ Message drafted that is honest and follows GDC and advertising rules for dental marketing
  • ☐ Message checked by a clinician before sending

Step 3: Service plans after treatment

  • ☐ Agreed who offers a service plan to suitable patients after their treatment
  • ☐ Team can explain simply how a plan helps patients come back regularly and spread the cost
  • ☐ Offer of a plan noted on the patient record after treatment
  • Team member leading this: ____________________

Step 4: Birthday offers

  • ☐ Chosen the birthday offer (for example money off a treatment): ____________________
  • ☐ Birthday list set to include only patients with marketing consent
  • ☐ Offer wording checked as honest and in line with GDC and advertising rules
  • Date we'll start sending: ________

Step 5: Pick your priority

  • The one idea that would make the biggest difference for us: ____________________
  • Why: ____________________
  • ☐ Posted our choice in the Growth & Patient Numbers discussion
