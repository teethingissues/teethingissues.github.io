AI Safety Checklist and 5-Line Team Policy Template

Work through the checklist with your practice manager or lead nurse, then fill in the policy template and share it with the whole team. Review it whenever you add a new AI tool.

Find out what's already in use

  • ☐ Asked each team member which AI tools they use at work (reception, nursing, clinical, admin)
  • ☐ Listed every tool mentioned: ____________________
  • ☐ Noted whether each tool is a free/public version or a business account
  • ☐ Checked whether anyone has pasted patient or staff details into a free tool

Check each tool before it's approved

  • ☐ Using a business account, not a personal free account
  • ☐ Checked what the tool does with the data you put in
  • ☐ Checked where the data is stored
  • ☐ Considered whether a data protection impact assessment is needed if personal data is involved (UK GDPR)
  • ☐ Recorded the decision: approved / not approved / ask first

Ground rules for everyday use

  • ☐ No patient-identifiable information in free, public AI tools: no names, dates of birth or clinical notes
  • ☐ Everything AI writes is checked by a person before it goes to a patient
  • ☐ Everything AI writes is checked before it goes into a record
  • ☐ Team understand AI can sound confident and still be wrong
