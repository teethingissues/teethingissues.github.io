AI & Google Visibility Checklist for Dental Practices

Work through this with your website and Google Business Profile open side by side, ticking what's already in place and noting what needs fixing. Re-run the ChatGPT test once you've made your changes and compare the results.

The ChatGPT test

  • ☐ Opened a new chat and asked "best dentist in [your town]"
  • Date tested: ____________
  • Were we mentioned? Yes / No
  • Practices that came up instead of us: ______________________
  • ☐ Repeated the test after making fixes (date: ________)

Website text AI can read

  • ☐ Key information is written as text, not just shown in pictures
  • ☐ Our town is named plainly on the homepage
  • ☐ Each treatment we offer is named plainly in text
  • ☐ Opening hours are written as text
  • ☐ Real facts included: team size, years open
  • ☐ Prices shown as "from £" where we offer them
  • ☐ FAQ section added, answering common patient questions

Name, address and phone everywhere

  • ☐ Practice name matches exactly on website, Google and directories
  • ☐ Address matches exactly everywhere
  • ☐ Phone number matches exactly everywhere
  • Places where details didn't match: ______________________

Google Business Profile

  • ☐ Profile is complete: hours, treatments, contact details
  • ☐ Recent reviews showing on the profile
  • ☐ Opening hours match our website
  • Next step to get more recent reviews: ______________________
