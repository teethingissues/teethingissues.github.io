5 Fixes Website & Google Profile Checklist

Work through each section with your website and Google Business Profile open side by side, ticking what's already done and marking the gaps. Then pick one gap to fix this week and come back for the next.

Fix 1: Words, not pictures

  • ☐ Opening hours are written in plain text, not only in an image or slider
  • ☐ Prices (or prices from £) are written in plain text, not only in a PDF or image
  • ☐ Treatments are listed in plain text on the homepage or a treatments page
  • ☐ Anything important in a slider or banner is also written out on the page

Fix 2 & 3: Say where you are, what you do, and the real facts

  • ☐ Opening line names the practice, the town, NHS and/or private, and key treatments
  • Our new opening line: ________________________________________
  • ☐ Years open stated: ______
  • ☐ Number of dentists stated: ______
  • ☐ Prices from £ shown for main treatments
  • ☐ How fast new patients are seen stated: ______
  • ☐ "Welcome to our friendly practice"-style fluff replaced with facts

Fix 4: FAQ section

  • ☐ How much does treatment cost?
  • ☐ Are you taking NHS patients?
  • ☐ What do I do in a dental emergency?
  • ☐ I'm a nervous patient. How can you help?
  • ☐ Is there parking?
  • ☐ Do you offer finance?
  • Other questions our reception team hears often: ________________________

Fix 5: Google Business Profile

  • ☐ Practice name, address and phone are exactly the same on the website, Google and other listings
  • ☐ The right primary category is selected
  • ☐ Up-to-date photos added
  • ☐ Every treatment listed
  • ☐ Replies posted to reviews
  • Person responsible for review replies: ______________
