Google Reviews Starter Plan for Dental Practices

Work through this plan with your reception lead in one sitting, ticking each box as you go. Pin the reply rules by the front desk so the whole team stays on track.

Get your link ready

  • ☐ Log in to Google Business Profile for the practice
  • ☐ Click 'Ask for reviews' and copy the direct link
  • ☐ Save the link somewhere the whole reception team can find it
  • ☐ Test the link on a phone to check it opens in one tap

Ask every time

  • ☐ Set up an automatic text to go out after each appointment with the review link
  • ☐ If automation isn't possible yet, agree who on reception sends the link and when
  • ☐ Draft your text wording: ________________________________
  • ☐ Check the text offers nothing in return for a review (no discounts, prize draws or gifts)

Get the team involved

  • ☐ Explain to the team why reviews matter: new patients read them, and Google and AI assistants use them to recommend practices
  • ☐ Launch a friendly staff competition for asking patients for reviews
  • ☐ Competition start date: ____________ End date: ____________
  • ☐ Team reward for asking (never for patients posting): ________________________

Reply to every review

  • ☐ Agree who replies to reviews: ________________________
  • ☐ Reply to every review, good and bad
  • ☐ Keep every reply polite
  • ☐ Never confirm someone is a patient or mention any treatment or personal details
  • ☐ Check for new reviews (how often): ________________________

Track your progress

  • Review count today: ________ Date: ____________
  • Review count in 30 days: ________ Date: ____________
  • ☐ Post both numbers in the Growth & Patient Numbers discussion
