Staff File Mistakes Checker: 5 Things Inspectors Find (Plus GDC Bonus)

Print one copy per staff member and work through their file section by section, ticking each item that is in place. Anything unticked goes on your action list with a date to fix it.

Staff member details

  • Name: ____________________
  • Role (clinical / non-clinical): ____________________
  • Date file checked: ____________ Checked by: ____________________

Hep B and DBS

  • ☐ Hep B blood test result on file (not just the vaccination card) – clinical staff
  • ☐ Antibody level (titre) is 100 mIU/mL or more, before any exposure-prone work
  • ☐ DBS is an Enhanced DBS
  • ☐ Disclosure fields read "none recorded"
  • ☐ DBS renewed within the last 3 years (CQC preference, not law) OR staff member is on the DBS Update Service
  • ☐ If DBS is missing or pending: "Awaiting DBS" risk assessment completed and on file

References

  • ☐ Two references on file, covering the last 3 years
  • ☐ One reference is from the current or most recent employer
  • ☐ Neither reference is from a friend or family member
  • ☐ If one employer covers the whole 3 years: second reference is from a different person at that employer

Contract

  • ☐ No other practice's name left in the template
  • ☐ No [placeholders] left unfilled
  • ☐ Signed by the employer as well as the staff member
  • ☐ Conditions-of-employment clause included, covering Hep B, GDC, indemnity, DBS and CPD

Induction and GDC

  • ☐ Induction signed by the person who ran it
  • ☐ Induction signed by the new starter
  • ☐ Signed section by section, not one tick at the end
  • ☐ GDC certificate on file (registrants)
  • ☐ GDC expiry read from the sentence after the person's name, not the first date on the page – expiry date: ____________
  • Gaps found and fix-by date: ______________________________________
