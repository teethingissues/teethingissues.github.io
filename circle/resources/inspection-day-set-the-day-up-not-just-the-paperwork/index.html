Inspection Day Plan: Setting Up the Day, Not Just the Paperwork

Keep this with your CQC folder and work through it as soon as an inspection is confirmed. Tick each item as it's done, and use the huddle section on the morning itself.

Diary and staffing

  • ☐ Diaries kept light, with fewer patients booked so the team isn't juggling production pressure and inspectors
  • ☐ Fewer dentists running clinics on the day
  • ☐ No difficult patients booked
  • ☐ Senior staff member in and free to walk with inspectors and find evidence. Name: ________
  • ☐ Back-up senior contact if needed. Name: ________

Diary blocks so standards visibly happen

  • ☐ Cleaning and zoning time blocked into every clinical diary
  • ☐ Emergency time blocked into the diary
  • ☐ Team told these blocks must not be overbooked or skipped

Emergency kit check

  • ☐ Emergency kit location confirmed today: ________
  • ☐ Oxygen location confirmed: ________
  • ☐ Defib location confirmed: ________
  • ☐ Emergency drugs location confirmed: ________
  • ☐ If anything has recently moved, every team member has been shown the new spot
  • ☐ Every person can point to oxygen, defib and drugs with no hesitation

Team preparation

  • ☐ Each person has read the questions an inspector is likely to ask their role
  • ☐ Each person can answer in their own words: confident, not reciting
  • ☐ Anyone needing extra support identified and paired with a senior colleague. Names: ________

Morning huddle (calm, short)

  • ☐ Reset nerves and remind everyone it's a normal working day, just lighter
  • ☐ Confirm roles, including who walks with the inspectors
  • ☐ Remind everyone of the emergency kit, oxygen and defib locations
  • ☐ Recap the light-diary plan and the blocked cleaning and emergency time
