Induction, Appraisal and Wisdom Trail Checklist

Use this to check your induction records, plan appraisals and confirm that each person's appraisal, PDP and CPD link together. Keep a copy with each team member's file and review it at every appraisal.

Induction records

  • ☐ Each induction area covered properly (for example GDPR, cross-infection and fire)
  • ☐ Each section signed by the new starter
  • ☐ Each section signed by the person delivering the induction
  • ☐ Completed induction stored in the team member's file

Appraisal tracker

  • Team member: ____________ Last appraisal date: ____________
  • ☐ Over 12 months ago? Appraisal booked for: ____________
  • ☐ Every team member checked and overdue appraisals booked

Annual appraisal (looking back)

  • ☐ Objective review of performance over the year
  • ☐ Reflection from the team member
  • ☐ Reflection from the appraiser
  • ☐ GDC scope of practice checked
  • ☐ Signed and dated by both people
  • Gaps identified: ________________________________

Personal development plan (looking forward)

  • ☐ Goals written straight from the gaps discussed at appraisal
  • ☐ Goals are SMART
  • ☐ GDC recommended topics included
  • ☐ Training needs listed
  • ☐ Target date set for each goal

Wisdom Trail check (Appraisal → PDP → CPD)

  • ☐ Appraisal gaps appear as PDP goals
  • ☐ CPD certificates added during the year match PDP goals
  • ☐ Any mismatches noted (it won't always line up perfectly, and that's fine)
