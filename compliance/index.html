Questions

What does dental compliance support from Teething Issues include?

Teething Issues keeps UK dental practices CQC inspection-ready all year. A former CQC Registered Manager reads the practice’s real documents line by line, gives every gap a named owner and a deadline, and chases it until it is closed. The service covers 14 areas: audits, cleaning, COSHH, CPD, decontamination, fire safety, medical emergencies, risk assessments, staff paperwork, servicing and maintenance, reception, surgery, CQC inspection readiness and the Registered Manager interview.

Who checks our compliance?

A former CQC Registered Manager with more than 15 years inside real dental practices, from dental nurse to practice manager. Not a borrowed consultant title.

What does CQC look at when it inspects a dental practice?

CQC asks five key questions about every dental practice: is it safe, effective, caring, responsive and well-led. Most dental practices are not given a rating like Good or Outstanding. Instead, the inspection report usually says whether the regulations are met, or whether not all regulations are met.

What should we do if our CQC report says not all regulations are met?

Start with the areas the report flagged, which are most often well-led and safe. Teething Issues turns each point in the report into a clear job with a named owner and a deadline, checks the evidence the way an inspector would, and keeps standards high afterwards so the same gaps don’t come back.

Do we have to do lots of paperwork?

No. We do the checking and chasing. Your team just gets clear jobs with a name and a date on each one.

Can you help if an inspection is coming up soon?

Yes. Tell us at your growth chat and we’ll start with the areas that matter most for your inspection.
