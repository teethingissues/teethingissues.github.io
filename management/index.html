Questions

What does the Teething Issues management department do?

The Teething Issues management department takes hiring, onboarding and the practice inbox off a dental practice owner’s plate. Teething Issues finds the right people for the practice, sets new starters up properly from day one with the right paperwork for their role, tracks staff training and renewals, and keeps the inbox clear with replies drafted for approval, so nothing slips.

Do you replace our practice manager?

No. We take the heavy admin off their plate so they can focus on the team and the patients.

Do you send emails without asking?

No. We draft the replies and you choose what goes out.

What does it cost?

It depends on how many areas you want help with. We always tell you the price first. Book a growth chat.

Is our data safe?

Yes. We’re registered with the ICO, work under UK GDPR, and agree a data processing agreement with every practice.
