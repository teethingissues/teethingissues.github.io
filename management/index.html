What does Teething Issues Operations do for a dental practice?

Teething Issues Operations is a practice support service that takes the day-to-day running of a UK dental practice off the owner’s plate. It covers five areas. The diary: cancellations are caught as they land and offered straight back out, failed appointments (FTAs) are followed up and rebooked, and 24-hour reminders go out with every reply tracked. Hiring: the job advert is written, applications are sorted and interviews are booked around the owner’s diary. Onboarding: each new starter’s DBS, registrations and training are chased in before day one. The inbox: every email is read, sorted into the right folder and given a drafted reply for approval. Admin: renewals, suppliers and to-dos are kept in order, with a short weekly round-up. Teething Issues is run by dental care professionals with 15+ years in UK practices, and works side by side with the practice team so everything settles in properly. The owner stays in charge of every decision.

How do you cut FTAs and fill cancellations?

FTAs (patients who fail to attend) and late cancellations are one of the biggest hidden costs in a dental practice, because the chair time is lost and rarely won back. Teething Issues cuts them in three steps. First, 24-hour reminders go out before every appointment and each reply is tracked, so the team knows who is confirmed and who still needs a call before the day starts. Second, when a cancellation lands, the slot is offered straight back out to patients who want an earlier appointment, so the gap is filled before it costs the practice. Third, every FTA is followed up and offered a new appointment, rather than being forgotten. In the example screen on this page, two cancellations and an FTA are refilled before the first patient of the day. The practice owner sees a full diary, not the chasing behind it, and the reception team gets time back for the patients in front of them.

Do you replace our practice manager?

No. Teething Issues never replaces anyone in the practice team. The practice manager stays in charge of the team and the patients, and Teething Issues takes the heavy admin off their plate: the diary chasing, the onboarding paperwork, the inbox and the renewals. In most dental practices, the manager spends a large part of every week on tasks that pull them away from the people they manage. Handing those tasks over gives them time back for the work only they can do, like supporting staff, handling complaints and improving the patient journey. Nothing changes without the owner’s or manager’s say, every reply is drafted for approval rather than sent on its own, and a short weekly update shows exactly what has been done. The aim is a calmer practice where the manager leads and the admin is simply handled.

Do you send emails without asking?

No. We draft the replies and you choose what goes out.

How do you set it up?

Each Teething Issues system goes in one at a time, so the practice team is never overwhelmed. For Operations, Teething Issues works side by side with the practice team: setting up the diary routine, the reminders, the paperwork each role needs and the inbox folders, then running them with the team until they are second nature. Nothing is dropped on the team all at once, and nothing big changes without the owner’s say. Then there is a review meeting with the real numbers, before and after, side by side, and the owner decides the next step. After that, Teething Issues stays with the practice, with support when it needs it and monthly check-ins. The price is always agreed before anything starts.

Is our data safe?

Yes. Teething Issues Ltd is registered with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO registration C2051112), works under UK GDPR and agrees a data processing agreement with every practice before any work starts. Patient and staff information is only used for the job the practice has asked for, is kept to the minimum needed, and is never sold or shared for marketing. Access is limited to the people doing the work, and the practice stays in control of its own data at all times. Emails and replies are drafted for the practice to approve rather than sent on their own, so nothing goes out in the practice’s name without its say. If a practice stops working with Teething Issues, its data is returned or deleted as agreed in the data processing agreement. Any questions about data can be raised at the free chat before anything begins.
