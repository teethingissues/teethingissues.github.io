Questions

What is the Teething Issues Sales Engine?

The Teething Issues Sales Engine is a growth service for UK dental practices that covers every step from the first call to treatment booked. The Sales Engine chases and rebooks every FTA, tracks every KPI every day, finds the leaks in the practice’s numbers and closes them, trains the team in sales when conversion drops, tracks progress, and helps the team overcome barriers like price, finance and patients who need to think about it.

Why is our dental practice's conversion rate low?

Conversion is usually low in dental practices because patients slip through at a few predictable points: FTAs and cancellations that never get rebooked, new patient calls that never become a booking, consultations that don’t turn into treatment, and treatment plans that go cold. The Teething Issues Sales Engine finds which of these is costing the practice most and fixes it first.

Is the sales training pushy?

No. It’s about helping patients understand their options and feel confident saying yes to the care they want.

Who does the training?

Dental care professionals led by a CQC expert, former CQC Registered Manager and expert growth business consultant.

What does it cost?

It depends on how many areas you want help with. We always tell you the price first. Book a growth chat.

Is our data safe?

Yes. We’re registered with the ICO, work under UK GDPR, and agree a data processing agreement with every practice.
