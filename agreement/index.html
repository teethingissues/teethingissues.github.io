3. Price

Schedule A: the services

Never Miss a Call (receptionist). A voice receptionist for your practice line that answers calls when your team can't, including at lunch and after hours; speaks multiple languages; spots emergencies and passes them on straight away; and sends your team a summary of every call. It doesn't give clinical advice: it passes emergencies to you or tells callers to use NHS 111 / 999 as you've set.

Digital Twin. We record you once and build your twin in your face and voice. Every month we write and make 4 short videos for your practice. You approve every word and see every post in your own approval hub before anything goes out. Extra videos are available at a price we'll agree first. Your face and voice are only ever used for your practice, and we label videos as made with AI where platforms require it. When the agreement ends, we delete your twin within 30 days unless you ask us to keep it.
