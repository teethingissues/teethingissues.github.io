Who is behind Teething Issues?

Teething Issues is a UK practice support company run by a dental care professional with more than 15 years in UK dental practices. The founder is a CQC expert, a former CQC Registered Manager and a growth business consultant for dental practices.

First, the person behind every plan has done the job: answering the phones when the front desk is full, leading a team through a Care Quality Commission (CQC) inspection, and growing a practice’s monthly revenue. Second, every system Teething Issues puts in comes from real practice life, not theory. Finally, the founder speaks to every practice before any work starts.

For example, when Teething Issues looks at a practice’s CQC paperwork, a former Registered Manager reads the real documents line by line instead of ticking a template. Across a career of more than 15 years, the founder has worked with 12+ practices across the UK.

Why does Teething Issues exist?

Teething Issues exists because most dental practices don’t need more advice. Practices need the work done. In 2026, the same four problems hold good teams back in practice after practice.

First, calls ring out at lunch and after five, so new patients book somewhere else. Second, treatment plans go quiet after the consultation. Third, gaps and failed appointments (FTAs) leave dentists with empty hours. Finally, staff files and CQC folders get finished the night before an inspection.

Teething Issues was set up so owners and practice managers stop carrying all of this on their own. The aim is a calmer practice, a fuller diary, and evenings and weekends back. For example, one empty hour for a dentist who earns £350 an hour is £350 the practice never gets back, and a few empty hours every week add up to thousands of pounds a month.

What changes for a practice that works with Teething Issues?

Working with Teething Issues means a calmer practice and numbers that start to move. First, every call the team can’t reach gets answered, day and night. Second, enquiries and treatment plans get followed up, with consultation conversion measured and aiming for 70%. Third, gaps in the diary get filled. Fourth, the Compliance OS keeps the practice in order for the Care Quality Commission (CQC) all year, with 481 checks across 20 departments. Finally, the owner can start taking full days off again, without the phone in hand.

For example, a private practice run by the founder grew monthly revenue from £80,000 to £125,000, an increase of more than 50%. Every practice is different, so Teething Issues starts with the practice’s own numbers and shows what is realistic before anything begins.

How is Teething Issues different from a dental consultant?

Teething Issues is a hands-on practice support company, not a report-and-leave dental consultant. A consultant usually hands over advice. Teething Issues puts working systems into the practice and stays alongside the team while the systems settle in.

First, each operating system goes in one at a time (the Growth OS, the Compliance OS and the Operations OS), so nothing overwhelms the team. Second, owners and practice managers stay in charge, and nothing big changes without the owner’s say. Third, Teething Issues never replaces staff: the systems take repetitive work off receptionists, nurses and managers, so the team spends more time with patients. Finally, Teething Issues works with the software the practice already uses, including Dentally and Software of Excellence (SOE).

For example, a practice worried about missed calls can start with the receptionist alone and add the Growth OS later.

Why is Teething Issues selective about which practices it works with?

Teething Issues is selective because the Teething Issues name sits behind every practice the company supports. The founder, a former CQC Registered Manager, speaks to every practice before any work starts.

First, Teething Issues looks for owners and practice managers who care about patients and about the team. Second, the practice needs to want to do things properly, including for the Care Quality Commission (CQC). Third, the team needs to be open to new routines, because systems only work when people use them every day.

Being selective means every practice Teething Issues works with gets full attention from people who have done the job. In practice, being selective also protects the standard each practice is known for in its own community. For example, the free 30-minute chat is as much about fit as about numbers, and there is no obligation on either side.

Where is Teething Issues based?

Teething Issues Ltd is a UK company (company number 17496042) supporting dental practices across the UK. Teething Issues is registered with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO registration C2051112) and works under UK GDPR.

First, before handling any patient details, Teething Issues agrees a data processing agreement with every practice, setting out what data is used, why, and for how long. Second, the practice always stays in control of its own diary and patient records. Third, Teething Issues never asks for patient data during the free chat or the free trial.
