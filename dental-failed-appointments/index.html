Failed appointments: the rules, and how to cut no-shows.

For dental practice owners and managers in England. What you can and can't do about missed appointments, and the simple systems that keep the diary full.

Can a dental practice charge for a missed NHS appointment?

No, not in England. The British Dental Association's current advice (updated July 2026, after the April 2026 contract changes) is that the NHS contract in England still does not allow practices to charge NHS patients for missed appointments. Practices could charge before the 2006 contract, but not since.

Can we remove a patient who keeps missing NHS appointments?

Not for a single missed appointment. The BDA says one failure to attend is not grounds for removing a patient from your list, but repeated non-attendance can reasonably be taken into account when deciding whether you can keep offering that patient appointments safely and effectively. Have a written process, apply it the same way to everyone, keep a record of each missed appointment and every reminder you sent, and tell patients about the process up front.

Can a private dental practice charge a cancellation fee?

Generally yes, if the patient agreed to it before booking. Put the fee and the notice period (for example, 24 or 48 hours) in your terms, show it when the appointment is booked, and keep it proportionate. Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, terms with consumers must be fair and transparent, so a fee that is hidden or out of proportion to your real loss is hard to defend.

How can a dental practice reduce failed appointments?

  • Send a reminder a few days before and again the day before, by text and email, and make it easy to reply to confirm or rebook.
  • Ask patients to confirm, and phone the ones who don't.
  • Keep a short-notice list of patients who want an earlier slot, so a cancellation becomes a booking.
  • Answer the phone when patients ring to cancel. A cancellation you hear about is a gap you can fill. One you miss turns into a no-show.
  • Track your failed-appointment rate every week, by clinician and appointment type, so you can see what is working.

How do I work out what missed appointments cost my practice?

Multiply the number of missed appointments in a typical week by the average value of those appointments, then multiply by the weeks you are open in a year.
