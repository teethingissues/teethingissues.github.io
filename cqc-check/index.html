Teething Issues
Free · 2 minutes · 10 questions

Would you pass CQC tomorrow? And who answered the phone today?

Most owners find out about CQC gaps when the inspector is already in reception, and about missed calls never. This check shows you both, in 2 minutes.

Part 1 · CQC
1Could you show an inspector every team member’s DBS, Hepatitis B status and GDC registration today, in one place?
2Is your medical emergency kit and drug list checked and signed every week?
3Are your fire, legionella and COSHH risk assessments all in date?
4Are your autoclave daily, weekly and quarterly tests logged, with failed cycles written up?
5Has anyone checked your practice against CQC’s five key questions in the last 12 months?
Part 2 · Calls and patients
6Is every call answered at lunch and after five?
7Do you know how many calls you missed last week?
8Does every missed caller get a call back the same day?
9Is every treatment plan that wasn’t booked followed up within a week?
10Do you see your calls, bookings and new patients each day, not just at month end?

Answer all 10 to see your score. “Not sure” is fine.
