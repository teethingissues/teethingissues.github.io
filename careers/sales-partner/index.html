Teething Issues helps UK dental practices run calmer, compliant, growing practices: every patient call answered, inspection-ready for CQC, the right staff, and a fuller diary. We're built by dental professionals, not marketers, and we're growing.

What you'll earn

  • 15% commission on every package you sell, on anything up to £10,000.
  • That's about £300 for a Starter package, £750 for Growth and £1,500 for Complete.
  • For example: 2 Growth practices a month = £1,500. 4 Growth practices a month = £3,000. No cap.
  • Paid within 7 days of the practice paying us.

What you'll do

  • Speak to dental practice owners and managers, by phone, email, LinkedIn or people you already know.
  • Book them onto a free 30-minute Practice Growth Review.
  • Once trained, run the review yourself and close the sale.

What you need

  • Confidence picking up the phone and having warm, honest conversations.
  • Your own phone and laptop, and a few hours a week. Work when it suits you.
  • A fit-first attitude: we turn practices down if we can't help them.

Nice to have (not essential)

  • Dental knowledge: you've worked in a practice (any role), sold into dental, or know practice owners.
  • Sales experience, especially selling services to small businesses.

Training is given

A ready-made pitch, a step-by-step call guide, practice calls with feedback, a demo phone line practices can ring, and your own booking link so every lead is tracked to you.

How we hire

We read every application. Shortlisted applicants do a short practice sales call with a pretend practice owner (about 10 minutes). The call is recorded and scored to help us choose fairly, and a real person makes every decision. Then a short chat with us and a two-week trial.

Pay and status (please read)

This is a self-employed, commission-only role. Commission is paid on our practice packages (Starter, Growth and Complete). There are no upfront costs, nothing to buy, and you are never asked to recruit other people. Working part-time hours, you can earn the equivalent of the National Minimum Wage or more (for example, one Growth practice a month pays £750). Self-employed people are required to register as self-employed with HMRC and are responsible for paying their own tax and National Insurance contributions.
