What is the free website review?

The Teething Issues free website review is a check of how easily ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google’s answer boxes can read, understand and recommend a UK dental practice. We read the practice’s public website section by section and give it a citability score out of 100: a measure of how much of the website these tools can clearly quote when a patient asks for a dentist. Most dental practices we check score around 40, and well-run websites usually score between 60 and 75. No real website scores 100. The report shows the score, the three fixes that will lift it most for the least work, written out in full and in plain English, and the headings of every other fix we found. It is free, it needs no call and no card, and it usually arrives by email within the hour.

Why does it matter if ChatGPT recommends my practice?

More patients now start their search by asking ChatGPT instead of scrolling through a page of links. When a patient asks for a family dentist, an emergency appointment or Invisalign in their town, ChatGPT names two or three practices and says why. It tends to pick the practices whose websites answer patients’ questions clearly: what treatment costs, whether finance is available, what to do in an emergency, which treatments are offered, opening hours and where to park. A practice that is not named in that answer is invisible to that patient, however good the dentistry is. Most practices, and most of their local competitors, have not started on this yet, so the practices that fix their websites early get a real head start in their area.

What happens after I get my report?

Nothing, unless you want it to. The three free fixes are yours to use, and many practices pass them straight to whoever looks after their website. If you would like the rest, book a 15-minute chat and we will walk you through what to fix first and how long it takes. Practices that would rather not do it themselves can have Teething Issues write every fix and put it live, then re-test with the same tools to show a dated before-and-after. To be honest with you: once the fixes are live, it can take up to 6 months for ChatGPT and Google to start recommending a practice. We re-test and tell you where you stand each time.
