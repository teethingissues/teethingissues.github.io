A real example

A messy job, handled

The first problem Mino solves is compliance, the one that keeps practice owners up at night. Here's what already happens when a certificate is about to expire:

  1. Before:A staff member's training certificate, a fire risk assessment or an equipment service quietly nears its expiry date. Nobody notices until a CQC inspection is booked.
  2. 30 days out:Bobby spots it in the records and emails the named owner of that item: "Heads up, this expires in 30 days."
  3. Expiry date passes, still open:Bobby emails the owner again and copies in higher management.
  4. Two weeks past expiry:It escalates straight to the practice owner as urgent, with the owner's name and what's needed.
  5. After:Every gap has a named owner and a deadline, nothing slips silently, and the owner only hears about the things that genuinely need them.
