What is an AI visibility audit?

An AI visibility audit is a check of how easily AI tools such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI answers can find, understand and recommend a dental practice. This newer kind of search optimisation is often called GEO, short for generative engine optimisation. Traditional SEO helps a practice appear in Google's list of links. GEO helps a practice appear inside the answer itself when a patient asks an AI assistant for a recommendation. Teething Issues runs two real measurements for every practice. First, an AI citability score out of 100, which measures how much of the practice website an AI tool can clearly read and quote. Second, a town-by-town check, which asks an AI assistant for the best private dentists in the practice's town and records whether the practice appears. Most practices have never checked either, and many score around 40 out of 100.

Why does AI visibility matter for a dental practice?

AI visibility matters because more patients are starting their search with an AI assistant instead of scrolling through a page of links. When a patient asks ChatGPT for a family dentist, an emergency appointment or Invisalign in a particular town, the AI tool names a small number of practices and explains why. A practice that is not named in that answer is invisible to that patient, however good the dentistry is. The practices AI tools recommend are usually the ones whose websites answer patients' questions clearly: fees, finance, emergency appointments, treatments, opening hours and location, written as complete, self-contained answers. The good news is that AI visibility is still new. Most dental practices, and most of their local competitors, have not started working on it yet, so the practices that fix their websites early have a real head start in their area.

No, and any company that guarantees 100 out of 100 should be treated with caution. The AI citability scale is a measuring tool, and it rewards a style of writing that no real patient would want to read, so real websites do not score 100. Well-run websites usually score between 60 and 75. Teething Issues guarantees something more useful: a real, measured improvement, shown with a dated before-and-after using the same tools, and a clear town-by-town record of whether your practice appears when patients ask AI assistants for a dentist in your area. Is GEO the same as SEO?

GEO and SEO are related but not the same. SEO, search engine optimisation, helps a website rank in Google's list of links. GEO, generative engine optimisation, helps a website get named inside the answers that AI tools such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI overviews write for patients. Many of the fixes help both: clear, complete answers to patients' questions, correct structured data, and a well-organised website. That is why every Teething Issues audit includes a free SEO health check alongside the AI visibility work. A practice can rank well on Google and still be missing from AI answers, and the reverse can also happen, which is why we measure both separately.
